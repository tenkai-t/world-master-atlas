## Hint coverage and evidence

`hint-coverage.json` is generated by tests using mode-aware clue prompt text. All eligible capitals (207 × 5 forms), terrain (118 × 3), and heritage (108 × 6) have 2–3 safe hints in these checks. This is structural/safety coverage, not a claim that every fact was newly researched or independently rechecked. Original description provenance remains on each object. Newly researched supplements: Paris, London, Prague; Taj Mahal, Serengeti, Bamiyan, Historic Areas of Istanbul. URLs and checked dates are in learning-schema.hintPolicy. No UNESCO registration metadata, photographs, licenses or geography data were overwritten.

Examples: Paris→Seine / riverbank monuments; Serengeti→grassland herbivores / migration of wildebeest and zebras; Gibraltar Strait→existing explanation of Iberian Peninsula–NW Africa and Atlantic–Mediterranean plus non-repeated coordinate-based position clues.

Coordinate hints are computed from existing Natural Earth city/terrain representative points and UNESCO listed component positions. Great-circle distance and initial bearing are rounded and explicitly labeled. They do not assert municipal membership, national borders, adjacency or nearest distance to an entire feature. Nearest-reference hints supplement cases with limited verified descriptive content; they are not a substitute for a full editorial review of each target. Historical supplements are factual Japanese summaries, not copied descriptions.
